Company background

Experience that turns ideas into garments.

A&E Garment and Advertising and Technology P.L.C. was established in June 2015 as a garment company specializing in professional services for governmental institutions, non-governmental organizations and society at large.

The company combines garment production, embroidery and industry experience to deliver practical solutions and lasting value to its clients.
